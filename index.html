03 · The Singapore Landscape

Asia's
Pinnacle
of Finance

Singapore is among the world's most competitive financial centres — home to 7,000+ MNCs, three major stock exchanges, and a thriving ecosystem of banks, family offices, and professional services firms. For Indian CAs, it represents the most dynamic career and entrepreneurship opportunity in Asia.
🏦
Banking & Finance
Singapore hosts over 200 banks and is the third-largest FX trading centre globally. ICAI CAs serve at senior levels in risk, compliance, treasury, and CFO functions across major institutions.
⚖️
Tax & Regulatory
With GST, transfer pricing, BEPS compliance, and the new Global Minimum Tax framework, Singapore's tax landscape demands world-class professionals — a space where Indian CAs excel.
🚀
Startups & FinTech
Singapore's startup ecosystem ranks #1 in Southeast Asia, with 3,800+ fintech firms. CAs are increasingly founding, funding, and advising the next generation of category-defining companies.
📊
Audit & Assurance
From Big Four to boutique advisory, audit and assurance remains the cornerstone of Singapore's capital markets integrity — and the bedrock of the CA profession here.
🌐
Family Offices & Wealth
Singapore's family office sector has grown explosively, with over 1,000+ single-family offices registered. Indian CAs are at the forefront of structuring, compliance, and governance roles.
🤖
AI & Digital Finance
AI is reshaping finance at speed. From automated reporting to AI-driven advisory, the Prof-Preneur CA is leading this transformation — combining technical rigor with entrepreneurial agility.

COVER STORY · IFRS
IFRS 17 at Full Sail: What Singapore's Insurers Must Act On Now
With IFRS 17 Insurance Contracts now fully operative across Singapore's insurance sector, finance leaders are navigating a transformed reporting landscape. The Contractual Service Margin — a metric virtually unknown five years ago — now sits at the heart of profitability analysis, dividend signalling, and investor communication.

For Indian CAs working within Singapore's insurance industry, this represents both a challenge and an exceptional opportunity. The technical rigour demanded by IFRS 17's three measurement models (General Measurement, Premium Allocation, and Variable Fee) requires professionals with deep actuarial-accounting fluency — precisely the profile that ICAI training cultivates.

Our analysis indicates that firms still relying on interim presentation choices made at transition may face restatement risks as IASB's post-implementation review gains momentum through 2026.
CA Nikhil Jain · Secretary, ICAI-SG
